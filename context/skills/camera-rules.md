# Camera Rules — OS LESADOS (TV Broadcast Simulation)

Technical lens and movement rules appended to every image prompt.
Goal: every frame must look like a real Portuguese daytime-TV broadcast, not cinema, not photography.

## Lenses
- Use studio zoom equivalents of **24mm–70mm** (standard broadcast studio zoom).
- Natural perspective, no distortion. Faces keep true proportions.
- Exceptions only:
  - **INSERT SHOT** (hands, monitors, props): a slightly longer focal length is allowed for detail isolation.
- NEVER ultra-wide fisheye, NEVER extreme macro, NEVER tilt-shift miniature effect.

## Movements (interpreted as framing energy in a still frame)
- **STATIC / TRIPOD** (default for wide shots and formal interviews): locked-off, symmetrical, stable composition. Implied camera: fixed pedestal.
- **SLOW DOLLY IN / OUT** (gradual tension or emotional reveal): subtle forward presence, subject slightly larger in frame than a neutral framing, shallow sense of approach. No dutch angle.
- **HANDHELD SHAKY** (ONLY for chaos / backstage / aggressive reporter scenes): slight frame tilt energy, looser composition, sense of urgency. Use only when the scene action describes chaos, running, or confrontation in corridors/catering zones.
- **WHIP PAN** (fast transition between conflicting characters): horizontal motion energy, one character sharp with directional blur on the frame edge suggesting a fast pan arrival. Use only when the scene describes a sudden entrance or clash.

## Prohibitions
- NO extreme worm's-eye (from the floor) or bird's-eye (top-down) angles.
- Horizon ALWAYS level. Verticals (doors, set walls, monitors) stay vertical.
- No drone shots, no crane shots, no 360 spins.
- The camera is an invisible studio camera: it never appears in frame, never casts shadows, never films mirrors showing the crew.
