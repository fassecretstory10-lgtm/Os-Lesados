# Framing Guide — OS LESADOS (News / Reality-Show Standard)

Composition rules appended to every image prompt.
Goal: technically serious TV composition that contrasts with the absurd satirical action (Looney Tunes meets CNN).

## Headroom
- Small margin above the head — the tight crop typical of TV. No giant empty space above characters, no decapitation by the frame edge.

## Lead Room
- Reserve space in the direction of gaze and movement. A character looking frame-right sits frame-left, and vice versa.
- In confrontations (OTS, two-shots), keep balanced air between faces.

## Standard Framings (match the shot label in the prompt)
- **WIDE SHOT (WS)**: full set visible + spatial interaction. Establishes where everybody is. All listed characters present and fully in frame.
- **MEDIUM SHOT (MS)**: waist up. Focus on gestures and dialogue. Hands visible when gesturing.
- **CLOSE-UP (CU)**: face fills the frame. Focus on micro-emotion (forced smile, sweat, raised chin). Background softly simplified but still the same location.
- **OVER-THE-SHOULDER (OTS)**: confrontational dialogue. Blurred foreground shoulder/head on one side, sharp face of the other character. Both characters recognizably the same identities.
- **INSERT SHOT**: object detail (hand on AC remote, ratings monitor, phone). Characters optional; location continuity mandatory.

## Satirical Contrast Rule
- Play the composition 100% straight: level horizon, clean thirds, professional broadcast framing.
- The comedy comes ONLY from the action, expressions, and situations — never from sloppy or "random" framing.
- Keep 16:9 broadcast framing with safe margins: no essential face or action element touching the frame edge.
