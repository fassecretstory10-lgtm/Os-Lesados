### 1. Dominant vs Secondary Palette
- Dominant: desaturated cool backgrounds (lab gray-green, spatial petrol blue ~55-65%); secondary: light/peach skin, brown hair, neon accents (portal green ~#00ff88, alarm reds).
- High saturation on characters, medium-low on backgrounds — figure pops from ground by saturation, not just value.

### 2. Outline Weight and Variation
- Thick uniform outer contour (~3-5px equivalent at 1K), pure black; thin inner lines (wrinkles, mouth, clothing detail).
- No inky tapered strokes; corners slightly rounded, never realistically sharp.

### 3. Lighting Patterns
- Flat shading with 1 cel-shadow step (simple shape, no gradients); thin cool rim light in space/night scenes.
- Hard cartoon cut shadows; minimal highlights (small round eye glints, rare streaks on metal/glass).

### 4. Facial Anatomy and Exaggeration
- Eyes: bulging white ovals with small pinpoint pupils; wide elastic mouth (extreme squash-and-stretch range in panic/scream).
- Conventions: gritted grid teeth, parallel stress-wrinkle arcs, single sweat drop, forehead anger vein.
- Expressions flip between total deadpan and maximum panic with no soft in-between states.

### 5. Scene Composition
- Loose rule-of-thirds: characters centered or slightly off-center; false depth via overlapping cels, occasional blurred foreground, simple gradient skies.
- Medium-low background detail with 1-2 focused nerdy elements (whiteboards, gadgets); typical framings WS for lab/garage, MS for dialogue, CU for reaction.
